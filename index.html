About

Researcher, founder, and digital innovator with a hands-on approach to solving workflow problems.

I develop software that reduces repetitive administrative work, strengthens accountability, and shifts organizations away from paper-based processes toward structured digital systems.

I completed my degree with First Class Honours in Automobile Technology Education at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi. My research examined the gap in OBD-II diagnostic skills among auto-mechanic students in North-Eastern Nigeria using surveys and interviews. That work revealed a broader pattern: technical and administrative systems often lack the digital skills, tools, and structured processes needed to function well. This realization pushed me toward software development, automation, data systems, and artificial intelligence as practical tools for closing skill gaps and strengthening institutional performance.
