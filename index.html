About

Researcher, founder, and digital innovator with a practical eye for workflow problems.

I design and develop software that helps teams cut repetitive admin work, improve accountability, and move from paper-heavy processes to structured digital systems.

I graduated with First Class Honours in Automobile Technology Education from Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi, where my research examined the OBD-II diagnostic skills gap among auto-mechanic students in North-Eastern Nigeria through surveys and interviews. The findings helped me see a wider challenge: technical and administrative systems need stronger digital skills, better tools, and structured processes to work efficiently. That insight shaped my transition into software, automation, data-driven systems, and artificial intelligence as practical ways to close skill gaps and improve institutional performance.
